\chapter{Hyperparameter Search Spaces}
\label{app:espacos-busca}

This appendix transcribes, for each tuned model of the benchmark, the
hyperparameter search space exactly as defined in the versioned
artifact \texttt{configs/\allowbreak models.\allowbreak yaml} --- the
single source the runner
(\texttt{scripts/\allowbreak run\_\allowbreak all.\allowbreak py})
consumes. Optimization follows the protocol of
Chapter~\ref{chap:metodologia}: Optuna with the TPE sampler and
MedianPruner, with an asymmetric budget of \textbf{100 trials} for the
GBDTs and \textbf{25 trials} for the deep-learning models, on the
internal 3-fold cross-validation (the justification of the asymmetry
is in the corresponding section of Chapter~\ref{chap:metodologia},
next to the protocol discussion).

Table conventions: ``int'' and ``float'' denote uniform sampling in
the indicated closed interval (on the log scale when marked ``log'');
``cat.'' denotes a categorical choice from the listed set. Parameter
names are the literal strings of the configuration file, for direct
traceability.

The two foundation models --- \textbf{TabPFN} and \textbf{TabFM} ---
are applied zero-shot and, by protocol definition, \textbf{have no
search space}: no hyperparameter is optimized per dataset. For
TabPFN, the subsampling-ensemble settings are in
\texttt{configs/\allowbreak experiment.\allowbreak yaml}; for TabFM,
the 50{,}000-row context cap and the ensemble size are fixed in the
wrapper
(\texttt{src/\allowbreak models/\allowbreak tabfm\_\allowbreak model.\allowbreak py}),
mirroring TabPFN's policy. This budget asymmetry (full search for the
others, none for the FMs) is part of the experimental design and is
discussed in Chapter~\ref{chap:metodologia}.

\section{Chosen hyperparameters per dataset}
\label{app:hp-escolhidos}

The following tables complete the traceability: for each tuned model,
the search's \emph{winning} configuration on each dataset --- exactly
what the final refit and the external folds consumed. No value is
transcribed by hand: the tables are generated by
\texttt{scripts/\allowbreak gen\_\allowbreak chosen\_\allowbreak hparams.\allowbreak py}
from the \texttt{best\_params} field of
\texttt{results/\allowbreak raw/\allowbreak <model>\_\allowbreak <dataset>.\allowbreak json}
(the English variants, by
\texttt{scripts/\allowbreak gen\_\allowbreak appendix\_\allowbreak en.py}).
Two readings they support: (i)~the search does \emph{not} converge to
a single configuration per model --- depths and learning rates vary
by orders of magnitude across datasets, which reinforces the need for
the per-dataset search protocol; and (ii)~values touching the edge of
the space (for example, \texttt{n\_estimators} close to the cap)
signal where a wider space could help --- a limitation discussed in
Chapter~\ref{chap:metodologia}. One reading note: identical winning
configurations across different datasets of the same model (it
happens with STab, MLP, and TabNet) are legitimate and expected ---
the \texttt{TPESampler} with fixed seed 42 proposes the same candidate
sequence per model, and when the same candidate wins on two datasets
the parameters coincide; the raw JSONs confirm. Notation:
\mbox{$a.b\,$e$-n$} denotes $a.b \times 10^{-n}$.
