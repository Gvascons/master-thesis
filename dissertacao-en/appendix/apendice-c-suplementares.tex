\subsection{Quantile sort repair}
\label{sec:app-abl-sort}

The text of Chapter~\ref{chap:destilacao} states that curve transfer
with sort repair ``acts as a calibration regularizer''; this
pre-registered ablation (2026-10-14; executed on 2026-10-15) isolates the
component. The same \emph{hard} and \emph{soft} quantile students of
the 20 datasets (3 seeds) were evaluated with and without the
post-hoc sorting of the 19 predicted quantiles. Quantile crossing is
the rule, not the exception: the median fraction of hold-out rows
with at least one crossing is 96.2\% (minimum 0, maximum 100\%). The
repair improves or ties the CRPS on 20 of 20 datasets in both arms;
strict improvement is 20 of 20 in the \emph{hard} arm (one-sided
sign test $p<10^{-5}$) and 18 of 20 in the \emph{soft} one
($p=0.0002$, one-sided --- the same directional convention of the
whole work --- with two exact ties: \texttt{online\_news} and
\texttt{pumadyn32nh}), and it brings PICP90 closer to nominal on 19
of 20 and 14 of 20, respectively. The median effect, however, is
small --- 0.7\% (\emph{hard}) and 0.3\% (\emph{soft}) of relative
CRPS, with a tail up to 28.8\% on the datasets where crossing is
most damaging --- so no conclusion of the chapter depends on the
repair: it is a consistent, zero-cost regularizer, not a source of
advantage. Source:
\texttt{results/\allowbreak distillation/\allowbreak ablation\_\allowbreak sort.\allowbreak csv}.

\subsection{Post-hoc conformal calibration (declared pilot)}
\label{sec:app-pilot-conformal}

A pre-registered exploratory pilot (2026-10-14, executed on 2026-10-15;
pilot $\neq$ result): if \emph{both} students receive per-quantile
split-conformal calibration (CQR style, with 10\% of the pool
reserved for calibration), does the distilled student's advantage
survive? On 5 datasets (3 of positive retention, 2 upsets; 3 seeds),
conformalization fixes the coverage --- on the criterion of the
distance to nominal decreasing --- on 5 of 5 datasets in the
\emph{soft} arm and 3 of 5 in the \emph{hard} one (PICP90 from
0.77--0.93 to 0.89--0.95 in the \emph{hard} arm and from 0.71--0.89
to 0.88--0.91 in the \emph{soft}; the exceptions are the
california-\emph{hard}, which already overcovered and whose coverage
it moves away from nominal, with a PICP80 distance from $8.06$ to
$9.63$~p.p., and the cps88wages-\emph{hard}, already within 1~p.p.\
of nominal, which moves by less than 1~p.p.), but leaves the CRPS
essentially intact (movements below 1\% in all 10 arms). The
distilled student's shape advantage survives in full
(\texttt{cpu\_activity} 1.18 \emph{vs.} 1.29; \texttt{cps88wages}
160.5 \emph{vs.} 161.0; \texttt{california} 0.108 \emph{vs.} 0.181
--- with the erratum's binarized-target caveat; the pattern holds on
the 4 remaining datasets), and the two upsets remain upsets.
Reading: conformalization buys \emph{calibration} and distillation
buys \emph{shape} --- they are complementary interventions, not
substitutes. A caveat declared before execution: in the \emph{soft}
arm, the teacher's OOF labels were generated over the full pool, so
the calibration split participated (via the teacher's context) in
the training targets --- a weak exchangeability asymmetry the
\emph{hard} arm does not have; an extension beyond the pilot would
require regenerating the OOF labels within the 90\%. Source:
\texttt{results/\allowbreak distillation/\allowbreak pilot\_\allowbreak conformal.\allowbreak csv}.

\section{Graveyard of hypotheses and deviations}
\label{sec:suple-cemiterio}

In the tradition of the transparency appendix of
\citet{grinsztajn2022}, we record here the hypotheses that were
tested and did not survive, and the process deviations that affected
the work's artifacts --- each with the rubric \emph{what it was /
what happened / why it is here} and the pointer to the primary
record. The research program's conduct contract requires negative
results to be reported and corrections to be made by dated
rectification, never by rewriting history; this record is the
consolidation of those episodes.

\subsection{H1 --- point distillation}
\emph{What it was:} the hypothesis that a distilled GBDT student
would retain $\geq 50\%$ of the teacher's RMSE gap on $\geq 3$ of
the 5 regression datasets, pre-registered in
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak desenho-\allowbreak experimental-\allowbreak destilacao.\allowbreak md}
(\S1). \emph{What happened:} refuted on the five central datasets
with the TabPFN teacher; the refutation replicated with the TabFM
teacher, of larger point gaps, and in the confirmatory extension to
the full 20-dataset pool (CTR23), in which the distilled point
student beats the true-label control on only 4 of 20 datasets.
\emph{Why it is here:} it is the most important negative result of
Chapter~\ref{chap:destilacao} (Section~\ref{sec:destil-ponto}) ---
at full pool, the student does not absorb the foundation model's
\emph{point} advantage, regardless of the gap's size; what transfers
is the distribution. Records:
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak desenho-\allowbreak experimental-\allowbreak destilacao.\allowbreak md}
(pre-registration),
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak relatorio-\allowbreak ai2.\allowbreak md}
(phases 1--2), Sections~\ref{sec:destil-ponto}
and~\ref{sec:destil-e1}.

The pool-size sweep that scopes this refutation --- and supports the
reconciliation with the point gains of \citet{jeong2026} via the
low-data moderator --- is in Table~\ref{tab:app-cap-sweep}: the
distilled student's point advantage appears on
\texttt{wine\_quality} with the pool capped at 800 rows and
disappears (or inverts) from 2{,}000 on; on
\texttt{california}$^\dagger$ there is a marginal $+0.005$ residue
at 2{,}000, gone at 8{,}000.

\subsection{Heavy tail as failure moderator (log-target)}
\emph{What it was:} with the extension to $N{=}15$, three of the
four distributional-retention failures concentrated on
heavy-tailed/price-scale targets; the live hypothesis was that the
$\log(1+y)$ transformation would recover the retention. \emph{What
happened:} the dedicated ablation refuted the hypothesis --- the log
does not rescue the failures (\texttt{fifa} $-1.69$); the additional
reading that it would ``collapse'' the teacher's advantage was
withdrawn in a recorded correction (it compared incommensurable
units --- in relative terms the advantage persists;
Table~\ref{tab:app-abl-log}). \emph{Why it is here:} the refutation
is more useful than the hypothesis --- it became clause (i) of the
``when to distill'' decision rule (Section~\ref{sec:destil-regra}):
before distilling, apply cheap target transformations. Records:
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak relatorio-\allowbreak ai2.\allowbreak md}
(phase 6a),
\texttt{results/\allowbreak distillation/\allowbreak ablation\_\allowbreak logtarget.\allowbreak csv}.

\subsection{Distillation by sampling (an original methodological variant)}
\emph{What it was:} a pre-registered exploratory hypothesis (H-X1,
2026-10-14) that sampling $K{=}8$ pseudo-labels per row from the
teacher's distribution (inverse of the OOF quantile function) and
training the quantile student with the native pinball loss on the
augmented pool --- genuine quantile regression instead of curve
imitation by MSE --- would match or beat the MSE student in CRPS on
the positive-advantage datasets. \emph{What happened:} refuted in
the 5-dataset pilot (3 seeds): the variant only beats the MSE
student on \texttt{california\_\allowbreak housing} ($+2.2\%$), ties
on \texttt{cps88wages}, and loses on the rest (down to $-5.6\%$ on
\texttt{cpu\_activity}), costing 2.8--44$\times$ the training time.
Sampling improves calibration where the MSE student undercovers
(\texttt{california}: PICP80 from 0.60 to 0.80), but the conformal
pilot (Appendix~\ref{sec:app-pilot-conformal}) shows the same
calibration can be bought at a fraction of the cost. \emph{Why it is
here:} it was the final round's candidate for an original
methodological contribution; the pre-registered criterion required,
in case of refutation, recording it as a tested variant without a
claim --- and that is what this record does. Post-erratum note
(2026-10-19): the variant's only win is exactly on the erratum's
binarized-target dataset; without it, the scoreboard is 0 of 4, and
the refutation strengthens. Records:
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak desenho-\allowbreak melhorias-\allowbreak finais.\allowbreak md}
(dated pre-registration and verdict),
\texttt{results/\allowbreak distillation/\allowbreak pilot\_\allowbreak sampling.\allowbreak csv}.

\subsection{Meta-feature routing}
\emph{What it was:} the promise, inherited from the meta-learning
literature, that descriptive dataset characteristics (meta-features)
could predict the winning family --- the decision framework would be
a predictor, not a flowchart. \emph{What happened:} under
leave-one-dataset-out validation, the depth-2 tree does not beat the
majority-class baseline in either model generation (hit rate $0.11$
\emph{vs.} $0.44$ with 11 models; $0.72$ \emph{vs.} $0.78$ with 14).
\emph{Why it is here:} the negative result is what justifies the
framework's final form (decision by constraints, not by prediction);
the tree remains in the work only as a descriptive illustration.
Records: Section~\ref{sec:resultado-negativo} of
Chapter~\ref{chap:framework},
\texttt{results/\allowbreak aggregated/\allowbreak lodo\_\allowbreak validation.\allowbreak csv}
and \texttt{lodo\_\allowbreak validation\_\allowbreak 14.\allowbreak csv},
\texttt{scripts/\allowbreak lodo\_\allowbreak validation.\allowbreak py}.

\subsection{The retracted claim of the ``deterministic'' OOM on
\texttt{year\_\allowbreak prediction}}
\emph{What it was:} during the Pareto-frontier measurement, it was
claimed that the TabPFN teacher point with a 50{,}000-row context on
\texttt{year\_\allowbreak prediction} was unmeasurable on the 16~GB
GPU due to a deterministic OOM. \emph{What happened:} re-analysis of
the execution logs showed the claim was wrong --- the culprit was
the automatic memory mode's estimate; with
\texttt{memory\_\allowbreak saving\_\allowbreak mode} forced, the
point was measured ($148.6$~ms/row, $\sim$3.3$\times$ the latency of
the 25{,}000 point, for an RMSE gain of only $8.661 \to 8.610$). The
claim was retracted per the correction policy, and the measurement
became an additional argument \emph{against} the full-context
teacher on 16~GB GPUs. \emph{Why it is here:} public retractions are
part of the conduct contract; the episode also illustrates that
``unmeasurable'' demands exhaustive verification of the operating
modes before being claimed. Records: the correction is in the
repository history, in
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak relatorio-\allowbreak ai2.\allowbreak md}
(phase 3), and in a comment of
\texttt{scripts/\allowbreak pareto\_\allowbreak strategies.\allowbreak py}.

\subsection{\texttt{diamonds}/\texttt{kin8nm} erratum}
\emph{What it was:} the \texttt{diamonds} entry of
\texttt{configs/\allowbreak datasets.\allowbreak yaml} pointed, since
the project's creation, to \texttt{openml\_id} 44980 --- which is
\texttt{kin8nm} (8{,}192 samples), not \texttt{diamonds} (44979;
53{,}940 samples). \emph{What happened:} the error was discovered on
2026-07-20, when the consolidated analysis of the CTR23 extension
flagged metrics identical to the 4th decimal place between
``\texttt{kin8nm} (extension)'' and ``\texttt{diamonds} (core)''; an
earlier clue (a 6{,}553-row pool, incompatible with $\sim$43
thousand) had been wrongly accepted as a configuration approximation
--- a recorded lapse. Experimental validity was not affected (only
the label was wrong); the labels were corrected by a rectification
commit with history preserved, the involuntary duplicate became an
end-to-end reproducibility test
(\texttt{extension\_\allowbreak kin8nm\_\allowbreak repro\_\allowbreak check.\allowbreak csv}:
numbers reproduced exactly under the same seeds), and the real
\texttt{diamonds} entered the extension as \texttt{diamonds\_real}.
\emph{Why it is here:} it is the most serious record-keeping
deviation in this work and the origin of two safeguards (name/ID/dimension
verification at download; an \texttt{approx\_\allowbreak samples}
divergence as a mandatory investigation signal). Canonical record:
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak errata-\allowbreak diamonds-\allowbreak kin8nm.\allowbreak md},
with the erratum and re-execution commits identified in the
repository history.

\subsection{Identity erratum of the regression core}
\emph{What it was:} the safeguard promised in the previous erratum
--- the name/ID/dimension verification --- remained pending.
\emph{What happened:} executed on 2026-10-19 over the study's 33
identifiers, it found the two remaining cases, both inherited from
the project's creation commit. The label ``superconduct'' had always
designated OnlineNewsPopularity (OpenML 44064; a legitimate
continuous log-shares regression) --- the config's old metadata
described the intended superconduct, never the loaded data; the
label was renamed to \texttt{online\_news} in all artifacts by a
versioned rectification script, with history preserved. And
\texttt{california\_\allowbreak housing} (OpenML 44090) is the
\textbf{binarized-target} variant of the \emph{tabular-benchmark}
classification suite: kept as executed (the 20-dataset population
was the one registered in the two-stage design
(Section~\ref{sec:destil-desenho}); removing it a posteriori would
be post-hoc curation), with an exclusion sensitivity analysis
attached to every headline result of Chapter~\ref{chap:destilacao}
--- sign test, E3, Friedman, A-SORT, R-COND, and A-GRID survive; the
one-sided Wilcoxon of the retention moves from 0.045 to 0.067; E6's
only win and R-TUNE's showcase case were on the degenerate dataset.
\emph{Why it is here:} it closes the cycle opened by the previous
erratum --- the lesson recorded there, executed, found exactly what
it predicted; the new recorded lesson is that curated suites reuse
the same name across classification and regression variants of the
same data, so identity requires checking the source suite. Canonical
record:
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak errata-\allowbreak datasets-\allowbreak core-\allowbreak regressao.\allowbreak md};
sensitivity:
\texttt{results/\allowbreak distillation/\allowbreak sensitivity\_\allowbreak california.\allowbreak csv}.

\section{Design record, searches, and licenses of Chapter~7}
\label{sec:app-registro-cap7}

This section concentrates the registry material that the body of
Chapter~\ref{chap:destilacao} summarizes and points to.

\textbf{Novelty searches (scope $\times$ dates).} The
distributional-distillation framing was checked against the
literature and public repositories in dated sweeps: 2026-07-15 to
2026-07-20 (initial sweep; versioned memo in
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak memo-\allowbreak novidade-\allowbreak destilacao.md}),
2026-10-04 (pre-delivery; it recorded the partial collision of
\citealp{jeong2026} and TabFM's technical report), 2026-10-14 to
2026-10-19 (post-erratum re-sweeps), 2026-11-05, 2026-11-12,
2026-11-17, and 2026-11-23 (the last).
The wider-scope sweeps (all the study's gaps, not only distillation)
are those of 2026-07-13 to 2026-07-20, 2026-10-04, 2026-10-14,
2026-10-19, 2026-11-05, and 2026-11-12,
recorded in the memo and in the repository history. One claim
correction belongs to this record: the July 2026 checks stated that
no independent benchmark of tabular KANs existed; the 2026-10-04
re-sweep identified \citet{poeta2024} as earlier (June 2024), and
the claim was re-scoped in a dated addendum to ``first test of the
family against the full grid of families under a multi-criteria
protocol''.

\textbf{Chronology of the two-stage design and sequential
correction.} The pre-registration of 2026-07-15 fixed H1--H3, the
post-pilot criteria, and the forecast of an 8-to-10-dataset
extension. The sign test and the eligibility rule (3--100 thousand
rows) were fixed on 2026-07-20, after an interim reading on 15
datasets (11 positives, $p=0.059$), and the population was completed
to 20. Treating the two readings as a two-stage sequential design
(nominal 5\% level at each): the procedure's global type~I error is
0.0283; the stage-ordered adjusted $p$-value for 16/20 is 0.0189;
the extension alone gave 5 positives out of 5 ($p=0.03125$); the
Bonferroni upper bound with two readings is 0.0118
(\texttt{scripts/\allowbreak interim\_\allowbreak sequencial.py}).
Execution deviations recorded in dated addenda in the design
document: a 50{,}000-row cap on the teacher's context (mirroring the
benchmark); block prediction under VRAM constraints; failure-tolerant
chaining; inherited hyperparameters in place of per-student Optuna
(cost measured by the re-tuning of the 20, addendum of 2026-11-12,
executed on 2026-11-17); the mixture variant executed only at
$\lambda{=}0.8$; the MUNGE transfer set not executed. The
dataset-identity corrections (\texttt{diamonds}/\texttt{kin8nm};
\texttt{online\_news}; the binarized target of
\texttt{california\_housing}) are in the canonical errata cited in
Chapter~\ref{chap:metodologia}; the accidental duplicated run of the
\texttt{diamonds} episode reproduced the pipeline end to end with
identical results.

\textbf{Teacher licenses (clause text).} TabPFN-2.5 (TABPFN-2.5
Non-Commercial License v1.1, checked at the source on 2026-11-23):
(i) outputs are not derivatives --- ``\emph{Outputs are not
considered Derivatives under this License}'' (\S1); (ii) the
licensed use is non-commercial, and training, fine-tuning, or

advantage; on one dataset (\texttt{pumadyn32nh}), a born-again
